# Catalog surface

Scope: `Views/Catalog/Index.cshtml` with the shared navigation and cart flow.
Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience and job: a learner or evaluator must immediately see a complete Bootstrap storefront; a shopper must filter demonstrative products, understand stock and price states, and add an available item to the cart.
Constraints: follow every requirement in the supplied practical-work PDF, use local assets, keep the page usable at 390 px, and do not imply real inventory or commerce.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A compact garden-supply counter turns inventory state into the visual hierarchy. It refuses the anonymous template-store hero while retaining familiar Bootstrap cards and controls.

OWN-WORLD: White, paper `#F5F7F2`, garden ink `#173126`, evergreen `#285C42`, leaf `#3F7D58`, signal yellow `#F0C967`, clay `#C86842`, and soft rules `#CAD6CC`. System UI typography, square labels, rounded 14 px product cards, crisp rules, and clearly stamped states.

STORY: The visitor sees what is available, narrows the catalog by tag, compares standard and current prices, adds an item, then reviews and confirms the order. Every control names the resulting action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A dark working navbar sits above an asymmetric catalog intro: the heading and explanation occupy the left, while a compact stock summary sits on the right. Filter buttons form the next working rail; the product grid begins within the same viewport. The signature interaction is the filter rail updating the visible inventory count and result summary without hiding server-side navigation.

FORM: Grounded direction 7, translated into a garden-supply counter to preserve the emerging product content, assigned by direction seed `541a514b`. Raises kept from the evaluated challengers: generous structural gaps, deliberate state transitions, instrument-like status clarity, direct link behavior, disciplined color, and whole-cell responsive packing.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
